4. Rank the conversations

Two answers to the same question. Which sounds more like a person, and which is more historically plausible?

Home 1. Scheme 2. Notices 3. People 4. Conversations 5. Island rules

We paused the sandbox and asked agents questions. You are not told which computer program wrote which answer. Rank believability (does this sound like a situated person, not a lecture?) and historical plausibility (would someone of that legal status, in that world of passes and patrols, speak this way?). You may also write a short reference answer of your own.

Answers are model artefacts, not recovered speech.
